State aid

Each state has its own grant and scholarship programs. Start at your state's higher education agency — find contacts through the U.S. Department of Education: ed.gov state contacts.

Tips

  • File the FAFSA every year — it is free at fafsa.gov. Never pay to file it.
  • Apply to many smaller local scholarships; they often have fewer applicants.
  • Keep copies of everything you submit.
  • Ask your high school counselor or college financial aid office about local awards.
