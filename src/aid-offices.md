# Financial Aid Offices

Your college financial aid office is one of the best free resources available — they can answer questions about FAFSA, grants, scholarships, and your aid offer. Education only.

## How to find your financial aid office

1. Go to your college's official website.
2. Look for "Financial Aid," "Student Financial Services," or "Bursar" in the menu or site search.
3. Look for a phone number, email, and office hours — many offices offer walk-in or virtual appointments.

## What to ask

- "What is my school code for the FAFSA?"
- "What is the priority deadline for institutional aid?"
- "Are there departmental scholarships for my major?"
- "Can my aid be adjusted if my family's finances change?"
- "How do I accept or decline my aid offer?"

## FSA ID help

You need an [FSA ID](https://studentaid.gov/fsa-id) to sign the FAFSA online. Create it at [studentaid.gov/fsa-id](https://studentaid.gov/fsa-id) — you and a parent (for dependent students) each need your own.

## Federal help

- [Federal Student Aid Information Center](https://studentaid.gov): help with FAFSA and federal aid
- [studentaid.gov contact page](https://studentaid.gov/contact)
