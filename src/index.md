## How to use this directory

1. Start with the [FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/fafsa) — it unlocks federal grants, work-study, and loans.
2. Browse the [scholarship directory](scholarships.html) and note deadlines in the [tracker](deadline-tracker.html).
3. Draft essays with the [essay help](essay-help.html) page and get recommendations early.
4. Contact your school's [financial aid office](aid-offices.html) with questions — they are there to help.
